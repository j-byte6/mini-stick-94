# Barnburner

A hockey tournament app for mini stick, roller or beer league nights. Live at https://j-byte6.github.io/mini-stick-94/

## Put it on your phone

Barnburner can live on your home screen like a regular app. It gets its own icon, opens full screen, and works at the rink with no signal. You can also tap **Add to home screen** on the app's main menu for these steps.

**iPhone or iPad (Safari)**
1. Open https://j-byte6.github.io/mini-stick-94/ in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up). It's at the bottom of the screen, or behind the **···** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open https://j-byte6.github.io/mini-stick-94/ in Chrome.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then tap **Install**.

Good to know:
- Tournaments and Cup records are saved on each person's own phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
- On iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own save, separate from Safari. Install it first, then run tournaments from the icon.
- Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's data, erases the saved tournaments and Cup records.
